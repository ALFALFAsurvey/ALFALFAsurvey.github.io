    UAT09.01 Scavenger Hunt #0: The Arecibo Telescope

    This scavenger hunt will explore some details of the Arecibo telescope and the Arecibo Observatory.

      0.0   Why do ALFALFA team folks always start enumerated lists with "0", not "1"?

      0.1   Below is a schematic diagram of the AO telescope as viewed from above. The 900 ton platform hangs in midair on eighteen cables, which are strung from three reinforced concrete towers. One is 365 feet high, and the other two are 265 feet high. The towers are named as if they were on the face of a clock oriented with "12" to the north: T4, T8 and T12. Which tower is the tallest of the 3?

      Note: this map predates the ground screen/upgrade.
