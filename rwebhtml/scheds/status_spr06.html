  • ALFALFA Spring Semester 2006: Planned Observations

    • Observations in the R.A. range from 07h30m to 16h30m in Spring 2005 focused on the tiles centered at +10 and +14 degrees. In spring 2006, we are completing these tiles as well as the one at +06 degrees; we may also try to start the one at +30 degrees.

    The graphical summaries below indicate the A2010 coverage during the spring 2006 observing season.

    Yellow indicates planned observations in pass 1.
    Green indicates completed observations in pass 1.
    Gold indicates planned observations in pass 2.
    Cyan indicates completed observations in pass 2.
    Brown indicates poor quality data (hardware failure, gain instability or unusual rfi)
    Pink indicates the area to be covered next (may be started but not completed in 2006)
