  • ALFALFA Fall 2005: Plans and Status

    • Observations in the R.A. range from 22h to 3h in Fall 2005 target the tiles centered at +26 and +30 degrees. We will update regularly the graphical summaries of planned and completed observations.

    Yellow indicates planned observations in pass 1.
    Green indicates completed observations in pass 1.
    Gold indicates planned observations in pass 2.
    Cyan indicates completed observations in pass 2.
    Brown indicates poor quality data (hardware failure, gain instability or unusual rfi)

    Note that the schedule displayed here is only approximate and may not correspond exactly to actual blocks of recorded data.
