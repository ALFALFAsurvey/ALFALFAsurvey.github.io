Description: (up to ~ 500 words)

The SMUDGES survey aims to generate a catalogue of dwarf galaxies that is unbiased towards their current rate of star formation. The result of this survey will be a catalogue of magnitudes, colors, redshifts and other optical properties of the detected dwarf galaxies.

Cross-correlation of HI detections made by ALFALFA survey with the optical properties determined by SMUDGES will be an excellent synergistic research project between the two surveys.

ALFALFA HI observations will provide independent confirmation of the redshifts of SMUDGES objects. Accurate redshift information will enable us to conduct follow up radio synthesis observations of selected objects, which can be used to investigate their kinematic properties. This ties in with the kinematics project that we are currently undertaking in order to investigate, among other things, the relationship between BCDs and compact dIs (cdIs). Specifically, I am working on a project where I compare the stellar, ionized gas and HI kinematics of a group of BCDs and cdIs to better understand their properties.

In addition, ALFALFA survey will provide accurate HI mass estimates for the objects in the SMUDGES strip. Since ALFALFA is designed to detect very low mass objects, mass less than about 10^8 Msun, it will provide valuable information on the environment of the objects and hence will help determine the influence of environment on the results of SMUDGES survey. Thus the resulting data set will be highly valuable in studying dwarf galaxy formation and evolution.

ALFALFA region: RA : 09:00 to 16:30 and 00:00 to 02:30   and DEC: +06:30 to +07:30
ALFALFA data to be used: Level II, ALFALFA Catalog  
Other data sets to be used: Optical data from SMUDGES survey. 
Products to be delivered: Production of Level II from Level I, HI and optical catalogs of SMUDGES galaxies, HI kinematics of selected targets.
