Catalogs of groups, grouping algorithms, etc.

Useful general references

  • Tully, 1987 groups in the "Nearby Galaxies Catalog". (This catalog is available in digital form.)
  • Huchra & Geller 1983, ApJS 52, 6. The HG catalog, based on the CfA redshift survey. This is paper III; see also I and II.
  • Garcia 1993 A&ApS, 100,47. Lyon-Meudon group catalog (LGG).

  • Heisler & Tremaine 1985, ApJ 298, 8. On methods of estimating the masses of galaxy groups.
