The drift 22p1 scheduled for July 6, 2005 is centered at
+05d13'54"; that means that Beam 0 (the central pixel), will
drift along that declination. The other beams are offset in
increments of 2.1 arcmin. So the 7 drift tracks will be located at:
     Beam 1  +05d20'12"
          6  +05d18'06"
          2  +05d16'00"
          0  +05d13'54"
          5  +05d11'48"
          3  +05d09'42"
          4  +05d07'36"
Remember than since each beam covers about 3.3 x 3.8 arcmin,
we may detect extended sources that are located somewhat to the
north of Beam 1 or south of Beam 4.

********   SDSS DR4   *****************
SDSS uses RA in degrees, so the boundaries are:
     RA = 200 to 225.0     5.0-5.45

Go to the SDSS SQL server:

http://cas.sdss.org/astro/en/tools/search/SQl.asp

SELECT
  p.ra, p.dec, s.z, p.expAB_r, p.fiberMag_r, p.petroMag_r, p.petroR90_r,
  p.lnLExp_r, p.lnLDeV_r, l.sigma, l.sigmaErr, l.ew, l.ewErr,
  l.chisq, l.nu, s.zWarning

FROM PhotoObj p, SpecObj s, SpecLine l
WHERE
  p.SpecObjID = s.SpecObjID AND
  p.SpecObjID = l.specobjID AND
  s.specClass=2 AND
  l.ew > 5 AND
  p.ra >= 200.0 AND p.ra <= 225.0 AND
  p.dec >= 5.0 AND p.dec <=5.45 AND
  s.z <= 0.06
order by p.ra
