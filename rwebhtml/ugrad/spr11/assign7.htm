0.   Scoping out "what to do"!

On Monday, Mar 14, you should discuss as a group what the fundamental science questions about Zw1400.4+0949, its galaxy population and its environment are that we might try to answer. Then try to tie those questions to the datasets we have available. Before the 50 minutes are up, one of you should email me with your collective thoughts.

My suggestion is that you first make up a list of science questions divided into (1) questions about how galaxies different one from another and how/why an isolated galaxy might be expected to evolve with time; (2) questions about how local environment might impact galaxy evolution (i.e. what processes are important) and under what conditions (i.e. when are those processes effective) will a galaxy's evolution be affected by its environment; (3) questions about the physical conditions in the environment in/around Zw1400.4+0949 and how they compare to conditions found in other structures in the universe. Then think about what fundamental properties of a galaxy you would like to measure (e.g. gas mass, stellar mass, linear size, distance, etc.) and how you can derive them from observables (HI line flux, optical magnitude, optical radius, redshift, etc.).

Of course, I don't expect your thoughts to be fully developed at this point, but let's see how far you can get; then we can think more about this together!

I.   Estimating galaxy properties from HI parameters

  1. What are the parameters that we can extract from the ALFALFA catalog? Be sure to give units.
  2. How can we estimate the distance to a galaxy from its redshift? What assumptions are involved?
  3. How can we estimate the HI mass of a galaxy from the HI flux? What assumptions are involved?
  4. How can we estimate the rotational velocity of a galaxy from its observer HI line width? What assumptions are involved?

II.   Understanding optical data

Main SDSS site         DR7 site        Imaging camera parameters and description
  1. What are the parameters that we can extract from the SDSS photometric database that we need to calculate interesting properties for comparison with the properties derived from ALFALFA? Be sure to give units.
  2. What are the effective wavelengths, in Angstroms, of the main broad-band filters used by many observatories: U, B, V, R, I?
  3. What are the effective wavelengths, in Angstroms, of the Sloan Digitial Sky Survey filters?
  4. How can we estimate the absolute magnitude of a galaxy? What assumptions are involved?
  5. What is "galactic extinction", and how can we account for it?
  6. What is the "K-correction"? Why is it necessary?
  7. How can we estimate the inclination of a galaxy? What assumptions are involved?
  8. How can we estimate the stellar mass of a galaxy? What assumptions are involved?
