Special topics for Nancy

 This summer, we're going to have you look at "high velocity clouds" in the ALFALFA data cubes. So, the first thing is: "What is a 'High Velocity Cloud' (HVC)"? HVCs are objects, seen mainly (or only!) in HI at velocities that are not predicted by simple models of how the Milky Way rotates but which are still within the escape velocity of the Milky Way. So, already, here are some questions:

  • What causes the 21 cm HI line to be emitted?
  • What are the characteristics of the gas clouds (temperature, density) that emit 21 cm HI line radiation? How are they different from "molecular clouds"?
    What are "molecular clouds"?
  • How are the Galactic coordinates (longitude, l, and latitude b, defined?
    What is the origin of this coordinate system? What is the galactic plane? In what direction is the Sun moving?
  • How does the Galaxy rotate? What is the Sun's motion?
  • What do we mean by "space velocity", "radial velocity" and "tangential velocity"?
  • Suppose you discover an object with a radial velocity Vr of -50 km/s in some direction l,b with |b| << 90. How could you estimate the object's distance?
  • What is the "escape velocity" of an object?
    What is the escape velocity of the Milky Way? How is that estimated?
