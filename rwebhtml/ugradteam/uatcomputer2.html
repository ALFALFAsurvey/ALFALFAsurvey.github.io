Undergraduate ALFALFA Team Computer Page

A computer running ALFALFA data processing routines must have the following minimum specifications:
  • Linux or Mac OS X operating system
  • IDL license
  • 4 Gb RAM
  • 500 Gb disk space
  • dual monitors, each with 1280 x 960 resolution or greater (desirable if not strictly required.) Large monitor, e.g. 1920x1200 pixel Macintosh monitor also fine.

Instructions to Obtain a Computer for your Institution
  • Have an institutional officer sign the computer purchase agreement provided by Union College: PDF or doc . Return the scanned copy of the form to Linda Clayton: claytonl at union.edu.

  • Consult with your campus Information Technology specialists to design a system that can be supported as much as possible by your institution. A warning: most institutions do not support Linux, so if you choose that option, you should make sure that the computer hardware is compatible with Linux.

  • Obtain a quote for your system. The total amount reimburseable by the grant is $3000. This includes an IDL license, should you require one.

  • Provide the information about your proposed system to Becky for approval.

  • Upon approval, there are two possible ways to purchase the machine:
    • (Preferred) Becky purchases with a Union College credit card and ships to you directly. In this case no reimbursement paperwork is required.

    • Your insitution purchases the computer/IDL license and is reimbursed by Union upon receipt of the invoice. (This may be necessary if your institution requires it and/or has a preferred vendor.)
