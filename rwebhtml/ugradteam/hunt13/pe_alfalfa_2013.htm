A quick look at some ALFALFA data and selected targets
Although we cannot conduct any actual observations in real time during class, we can simulate what an observing run of ALFALFA followup observations would be like using the off-line version of CIMA, the Arecibo telescope control and data acquisition system.

And, since we have already acquired a lot of data, we can look at some of it!

2.   On the night of 12.03.27, we detected a source at 1421 MHz. It is very strong, but it is confused with the Galactic hydrogen emission. Here is the entry from the logfile:
L093453.4+170305  093453.4 +170305  j   100 H v   # A198305   12.03.26   0925    2.7    35    208700297     Not Detected
Here is the high resolution LBW spectrum


Click on the image to see a larger version.

Oops! Our observers, not knowing what to look for (deliberately, we don't tell them what to expect), didn't realize there was an extragalactic source!
