Undergraduate ALFALFA Team Workshop


Target audience: Undergraduate ALFALFA Team members

Goals:

  • Instruct undergraduates (and their faculty mentors) about the ALFALFA project and followup projects and the nature of large legacy style surveys
  • Interaction of undergraduates with their peers, ALFALFA experts, and National Observatory staff
  • Presentations by undergraduates of their ALFALFA-related research
  • Observing experience on National Observatory telescopes
  • Provide a framework for exchanging materials (lecture PPT, handouts, exercises) among participants as well as the educational community.

Workshop Components:

  • Lectures covering the science background and technical details of ALFALFA
  • Tours of National Observatory
  • Observing run on facility telescope:
  • Faculty discussion of educational materials
  • See schedules at workshop links below
