Using SDSS, DSS, and NED

Below, we provide links to the entries for 5 interesting galaxies in several public extragalactic databases: the 7th and 9th release of the Sloan Digital Sky Survey (DR7 is a faster link), the SkyView Digital Sky Survey 1, the Digital Sky Survey 2 Red and Blue, and the NASA Extragalactic Database (NED). Note that the links to DSS1 and DSS2 are provided both at image sizes of 0.03 degrees and 0.1 degrees; for the SDSS image, experiment with the Navigator Tool to learn to zoom in and out, recenter, etc. The NED link provides you with a list of all entries in that database within 10 arcmin around the chosen position. We will discuss more about these images after you look at them!
