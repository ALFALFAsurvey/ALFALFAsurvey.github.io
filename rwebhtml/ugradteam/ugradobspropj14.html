Undergraduate ALFALFA Workshop

January 12-16, 2014

Workshop Observing Plans
  • The 2014 UAT Workshop will target Undergraduate ALFALFA Team Group Galaxies using the L-Band Wide Receiver, pending approval from the Arecibo Telescope Allocation Committee.
  • Visit the summary of upcoming observing page for updated schedules as they become available.

The Student Observing Proposal

 Part of the workshop experience is writing a proposal for the workshop observations. As a student, you are responsible to:
  • review material listed on the Things to Know page.
  • join telecons, as scheduled below. Each telecon will have background reading and a series of questions. The telecons are designed to prepare you for writing the telecon and the answers to the questions will help form text for the proposal.
  • contribute to writing and proofing the proposal draft

Proposal writing documents
  • A skeletal LaTeX file.
  • AO telescope schedules.
  • NAIC proposal instructions. We will not follow this 100%. Most notably, you won't submit the proposal and it won't be refereed!
  • The A2010 page at NAIC contains observing information. This is a good place to look for information on what has been observed already, and you'll also find useful things like the "LBW Observing Cookbook."
