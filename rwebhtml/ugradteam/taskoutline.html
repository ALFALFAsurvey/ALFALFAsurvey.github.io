Things to do to get science out of ALFALFA


Available datasets

  • ALFALFA grids
    Martha will provide you with the ALFALFA Level II data products corresponding to several ALFALFA grids which cover the region of your target group. These will include the grids themselves as well as the "src" and "ps" files and that source catalog. So the first thing to do is to learn what all these things are, what they contain, and how to use them. Among other things, learn to use "gridview" and "galcat".

  • The AGC
    Your IDL installation comes with a recent version of the AGC the misuse of which will insure you a free delivery of a pair of cement shoes. Make sure you understand what the AGC is (and isn't!). Among other things, learn to use "agcbrowse" and "plotalfa".

  • The SDSS
    In order to be able to understand the history and environment of the galaxies that ALFALFA does and does not detect, it is really useful to study the same region using the SDSS. So learn to use it and understand what it contains.

Things we need to do

  • Understand ALFALFA's limitations
    • What are the limitations of ALFA's angular resolution? What is the **linear** angular resolution at the distance of your group?
    • Why might you "find" a galaxy in a grid but not in the ALFALFA catalog?
    • What are sidelobes? What impact can sidelobes have?

  • Determine group membership
    • Using the AGC and NED to identify structures
    • See Giovanelli et al 1997 (Figures 2+) and apply their method on your group/cluster.

  • Calculate distances
    • Understand when and why Hubble's law doesn't work!
    • Understand what primary and secondary distance indicators are.
    • Understand what the velocity frame of the CMB is and how it is determined.
    • Understand what a peculiar velocity is.
    • Understand what a flowmodel is.
    • The EGG flowmodel routines (coming soon)

  • Correct optical magnitudes for various effects
    • Correct for galactic extinction
      • Understand what galactic extinction is and what methods are used to derive it.
      • Understand the DIRBE instrument and data set and how it can be used for galactic extinction.
      • The EGG DIRBE routine (coming soon)
    • Correct for internal extinction
      • Understand what the correction for internal external extinction is.
    • Correct for K-correction
      • Understand what the K-correction is.
      • KCORRECT courtesy of Mike Blanton et al. but interpreted for ALFALFA (coming soon)

  • Find the X-ray data
    • RASSCALS
    • Other
    • What does X-ray tell us?

  • HI deficiency references
    • Haynes & Giovanelli 1984

    • Solanes et al. 2001

  • Calculate properties
    • Distances
    • HI masses
    • Optical luminosities
    • Global colors
    • Stellar masses
    • Radio continuum luminosities
    • Starburst or AGN?
    • Local environment
      • Nearest Nth neighbor

      • Local ICM temperature
