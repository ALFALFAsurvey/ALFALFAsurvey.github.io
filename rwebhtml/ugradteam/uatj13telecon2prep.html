    Questions

      Survey Questions:


    1. How are the optical counterparts of ALFALFA sources determined?

    2. What is the difference between Code 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, and 9 targets in the ALFALFA cataloging scheme?

    3. What might the Code 1 sources without optical counterparts be?

    4. What is an OH megamaser?

    5. What is meant by "completeness", "sensitivity" and "reliability"?

      6. Observing Questions:


    6. What is the latitude of Arecibo Observatory?

    7. Suppose we choose to observe a source with a declination of +12o10'35" while it is on the meridian. We choose an azimuth angle of 360. What zenith angle would we use?

    8. What will the (approximate) right ascension of the position of the OFF-source scan be when we observe the source HI111307.9+291615?

    9. What is meant by RFI?


    For the proposal:

    Write a brief summary about each of the Observing Targets targets. Consider:
    1. HI characteristics, e.g., what does the spectrum look like? (double-horned? Gaussian?) What is the S/N?
    2. What does the code for each object mean? Did the cataloger think there was a plausible optical counterpart?
    3. Do you see a plausible optical counterpart for the source? Is there any spectral information in the SDSS that would tell you whether the source is near another galaxy in space?
    Prepare some figures showing information about the targets or a subset of the targets.

    Answers will be discussed in the Second Observing Proposal Planning Telecon
