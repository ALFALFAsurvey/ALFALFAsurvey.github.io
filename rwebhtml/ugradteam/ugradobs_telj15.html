Pre-Workshop Activities and Telecons

Workshop Observing Plans
  • The 2015 UAT Workshop Observations will search for low-mass gas-rich galaxies using the L-Band Wide Receiver, pending approval from the Arecibo Telescope Allocation Committee.
  • Visit the summary of upcoming observing page for updated schedules as they become available.

Student Workshop Preparation

 As a student attending the workshop, you are responsible to:
  • review material listed on the Things to Know page.
  • join telecons, as scheduled below. Each telecon will have background reading and a series of questions. The telecons are designed to prepare you for the workshop activities.
  • Contribute to group documents to answer telecon questions.
