Summer Research Budget Guidelines Page


Seven summer stipends will be awarded each year to members of the UAT.

The deadline for Summer 2010 is 02-26-2010 ANYTIME UT


We have 8 awards for 2010. Each award is for a maximum of $4500 but please spend less if you can!
  1. The grant can be used for student salary and student housing. We wish to pay the "going rate" for your institute. If you do not need the maximum award the remaining portion of the award will be used to fund additional students so that they can participate in the collaboration. If you use an hourly rate or base pay we recommend that it is equivalent to the average rate for YOUR institute. Similarly the housing allowance should be based on the local rate. Please state in your budget justification what data was used to determine the wage and accomodation rates.
  2. GSU will issue each institute a sub-award agreement that is cost reimbursable. If your sponsored research wishes to contact GSU prior to the submission of the summer research proposal please ask them to email Melissa Nease.
