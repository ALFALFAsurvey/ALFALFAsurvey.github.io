Academic Year Fall 2014 Research Page


Awards for Academic Year Spring 2014 Research will be made by August 15, 2014.

Students and faculty mentors should together prepare the following requirements for the application:

  1. Cover page
  2. Two page research proposal that includes
    • Student name
    • Title of project
    • Abstract up to 150 words
    • Overview of project and how it relates to existing ALFALFA or Undergraduate ALFALFA Team projects (include citations where appropriate)
    • Project gaols: outline what you hope to accomplish during the project
    • Summary of previous experience: e.g., ALFALFA experience, relevant coursework
    • Future conferences and papers: outline plans for presenting your results, e.g., AAS, UAT workshop at Arecibo, presentation at your institution.
  3. One page budget and budget narrative, following the Budget guidelines. The budget must be reviewed and approved by your Sponsored Program Office, and submitted with a letter of committment (template) signed by an SPO officer or other appropriate financial representative of your institution.
  4. Faculty letter of support

Mentors: please submit the complete application to Becky.
