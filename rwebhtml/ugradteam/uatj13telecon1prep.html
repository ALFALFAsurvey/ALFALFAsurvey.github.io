Preparation for UAT 2013 Workshop Observing Proposal Planning Telecon

Background Reading

  • Review your understanding of the equatorial coordinate system (right ascension and declination). See first bullet in Things to Know for help.
  • Review the Introduction to Arecibo Observing
  • Review the ALFALFA summary of 21-cm radiation, describing the 3 things the 21-cm spectrum tells you about a galaxy. Also review Hubble's Law.
  • Read about the ALFALFA survey.
  • Background reading on Dark Galaxies in ALFALFA

Questions

    Science Questions:

  1. Write a short summary about the ALFALFA survey. What are the goals? What has been found so far?
  2. What is the "missing satellite" problem? What is meant by "satellite" in this context and why do we think they are "missing"?
  3. How would we know a dark galaxy if we saw one? What is the definition of a "dark galaxy"? Do "dark galaxies" exist? Should they?
  4. Are all sources without optical detections likely to be dark galaxies? To help you answer this question, check out this press release and the description of ALFALFA observations of Virgo HI21 for an example. See also John Hibbard's HI Rogues Gallery.

    Observing Questions:

  5. What does "L-band" mean?
  6. The ALFALFA survey is using the ALFA detector. But we plan to followup exciting ALFALFA detections using the L-Band Wide (LBW) detector. Why don't we use ALFA?
  7. What is the frequency range covered by the L-Band Wide (LBW) detector? What wavelengths does this correspond to? Assuming that you were observing the 21-cm line, what range of velocities and redshifts does this correspond to? Compare to the coverage of ALFA.

Answers will be discussed in the First Observing Proposal Planning Telecon
