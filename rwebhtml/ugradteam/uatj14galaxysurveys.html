Galaxy Surveys/Databases for UAT Workshop Observing

Follow these links to find and learn about various useful galaxy databases and websites. The more you know, the more you will understand. And, the more you will be able to contribute to the ALFALFA research effort on your own.

  • SDSS, the Sloan Digital Sky Survey: an optical survey of the sky.
  • SDSS Navigator Tool
  • SDSS Data Release 10
  • Comparison of ALFALFA and SDSS Coverage

  • Skyview Finding Chart Tool

  • NED(NASA Extragalactic Database): searchable database of galaxy information

Exercise on galaxy surveys: Using SDSS, DSS2 and NED to find images/info of some interesting objects
