Examples of targets for the Jan 2014 observing run

During the Jan 2014 workshop, we will be conducting observations with L-band wide of galaxies in groups that show evidence of star formation, but were not detected by ALFALFA. Here you will explore a few examples of such targets. (Before you tackle the targets below, make sure you have done the Using SDSS, NED exercise.)

For each example target galaxy below, you are provided with:
(1) Link to image of R (stars) and Hα (star formation) obtained at Kitt Peak
(2) links to public database images and the NED.
Use this information to understand why we want to reobserve these and similar sources.

For comparison, check out a spiral galaxy with "normal" star formation and an ALFALFA detection:

For each galaxy:

  • Examine the R and Hα images, the SDSS images, and the SDSS spectra. Describe what you see.
  • What interesting features do you see in the images? How do these targets compare with the "normal" galaxy?

Some questions (to get you thinking):

  • What difficulties might we have in detecting HI in these sources? (Hint: what is the beam size, or diffraction limit, of LBW at 21-cm?)
  • Several of the sources are in the same group. Which ones are they and what group is it?
  • What questions do you have about these sources and why we want to observe them???
