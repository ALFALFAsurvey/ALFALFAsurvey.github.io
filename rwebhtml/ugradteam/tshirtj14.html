Design the Workshop T-shirt Contest

Instructions

Submit designs for our Workshop T-shirt to Becky by Wednesday, Nov. 14. We will select the winning design and have the T-shirts made for our workshop. The winner will receive a $30 gift certificate for the Observatory store (which has lots of nice Arecibo T-shirts and souvenirs). You can submit designs as a team or individually.

Some design hints:

  • Keep it simple
  • Limit to 2-3 colors including the T-shirt color
  • Try to use high resolution graphics (otherwise the company will need to digitize the image)
