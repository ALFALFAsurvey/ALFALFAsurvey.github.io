Student Workshop Preparation

 As a student attending the workshop, you are responsible to:
  • review material listed on the Things to Know page.
  • work on Scavenger Hunt #0. Find your Team members.
  • install topcat on a computer you will bring to the workshop (see installation notes on the topcat home page).
  • make sure you can connect your computer to the internet with an ethernet cable and bring all necessary equipment with you. Please also bring a usb stick if you have one.
  • be aware of travel requirements as listed at the Travel Information page
