The NSF-sponsored Undergraduate ALFALFA Team program promotes undergraduate research within the ALFALFA project.

The Undergraduate Team Groups Project

Nearby groups of galaxies provide interesting environments for studies of the impact of environment on gas content, morphology, etc. Some members of the Undergrad ALFALFA team are focusing on studying individual groups included in the ALFALFA volume.

This work is just getting started in Spring 2008. Stay tuned.
