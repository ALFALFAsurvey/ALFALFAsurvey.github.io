The Undergrad ALFALFA Team at Arecibo, August 2010


During Aug 17-24, 2010, 3 faculty and 6 undergraduate students from Hartwick College, St. Mary's College of California, and Union College conducted the ALFALFA (Arecibo observing program A2010) observations from on site at Arecibo. Students and faculty learned the observing and data reduction procedures. Each college was responsible for two nights of observing and one night of data reduction ("flagging"). Photos below show some of the highlights! Also check out Catherine Weigel's Post on the ALFALFA blog.
