-- The idea is that we would propose to observe all of the galaxies in the NASA-Sloan Atlas which lie within some metric radial distance (e.g. 6 Mpc?) and velocity range (± 3σ) of the cluster center and which are not detected by ALFALFA. Or, perhaps we should stick to all the **blue** galaxies in that region. Add also any galaxies with detectable Hα emission in the KPNO images. Or Hα EW in SDSS spectroscopy? Need to see how many galaxies we are talking about for each cluster/group.


Science Justification:
  • Poorer groups/clusters than previous studies (MPH/RG+; Solanes+)
  • Makes use of existing ALFALFA, N-SAtlas (GALEX-SDSS) and Halpha (UAT) imaging.
  • All have X-ray measurements (detections or limits); span a range of X-ray luminosities
  • Need to extend to lower HI fluxes than ALFALFA in order to probe gas-poor galaxies which are *not* detected by ALFALFA.
