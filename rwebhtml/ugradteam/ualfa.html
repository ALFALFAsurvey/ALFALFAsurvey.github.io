First ALFALFA Undergraduate Workshop
July 6-7, 2005
Union College, Schenectady, NY

Purpose:
  • To provide the undergraduates involved in summer research related to ALFALFA lectures covering the science background and technical details, to exposure them to the nature of large legacy style surveys and to give them the opportunity to exchange their experiences with their peers
  • To provide the framework for exchanging materials (lecture PPT, handouts, exercises) among participants as well as others.
Target audience: Undergraduates involved in E-ALFA research at nearby colleges and universities

Special Event: Remote Arecibo observation on Wednesday July 6
