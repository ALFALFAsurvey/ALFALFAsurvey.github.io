ALFALFA in the Leo Region: Looking for Missing Satellites in HI

Sabrina Stierwalt (Cornell) and the ALFALFA Consortium

The proximity of two nearby galaxy groups in the Leo region, namely Leo I at 10.4 Mpc and the slightly more distant Leo II, allows for a detailed study of low-mass galaxies. Leo I offers a uniquely intermediate density environment with both a low velocity dispersion (~130 km/s) and a local density enhancement high enough to support the presence of E/S0 galaxies. A catalog of HI line detections in the Leo region has been made from the blind HI survey ALFALFA. More sensitive, single-pixel Arecibo observations of Leo dwarfs that targeted candidates noted optically by Karachentsev and Karachentseva were also obtained. When star formation activity is very low and difficult to determine optically, sites of star formation can often still be identified by the faint blue light they emit in the UV. First results from pointed GALEX observations of the same Leo dwarfs targeted both optically and in HI will be presented.
