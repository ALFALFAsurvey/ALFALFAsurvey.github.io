ALFALFA HI Detections of Virgo Early-Type Dwarfs

Rebecca Koopmann (Union College/NAIC) and the ALFALFA Consortium

Early-type dwarf galaxies dominate cluster populations, but their formation and evolutionary histories are poorly understood. This poster presents a sample of early-type Virgo Cluster dwarf galaxies that have gas reservoirs as detected in the ALFALFA (Arecibo Legacy Fast ALFA) HI survey. Many of these galaxies show evidence for ongoing/recent star formation, as judged by H-alpha imaging and SDSS colors and spectra. These galaxies may be undergoing morphological transition due to cluster environmental effects.
