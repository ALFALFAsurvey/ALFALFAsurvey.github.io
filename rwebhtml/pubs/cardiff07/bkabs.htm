"Dark" Galaxies in the Virgo Cluster

Brian R. Kent (Cornell) and the ALFALFA Consortium

The Arecibo Legacy Fast ALFA survey has yielded a complete HI dataset of the Virgo Cluster and its environs (Giovanelli et al. 2007, Kent et al., in preparation). Assuming a distance to Virgo of 16.7 Mpc, the minimum detectable HI mass is of order 2 x 107 solar masses. A number of the HI detections appear to have interesting properties. Some appear associated with, but offset from, low surface brightness optical counterparts; others appear tidally related to more substantial offset optical counterparts. Another interesting set of detections are not identifiable with any optical counterparts. We will present the ALFALFA results on these objects in the Virgo region, as well as aperture synthesis observations obtained with the VLA.
