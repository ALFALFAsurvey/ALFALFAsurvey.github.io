ALFALFA at Brera: Agenda

May 30, 2005



A workshop to discuss ALFALFA results, issues and followup science for the convenience of dwellers of the Mediterranean coast was held at the Osservatorio di Brera in Milano on June 30, 2005. A list of participants is here.

The agenda was very similar to that of the Ithaca workshop in June, so look there for more complete material.
