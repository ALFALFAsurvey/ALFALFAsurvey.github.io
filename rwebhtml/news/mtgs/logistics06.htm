2006 ALFALFA at Ithaca

Fri-Sat June 23-24, 2006


Participants are responsible for making their own travel/lodging arrangements. A block of rooms is available at the Best Western University Inn; identify yourself with "ALFALFA" to get a group rate. The BW is about 1 mile from campus and provides shuttle service both from/to the Ithaca airport and to/from campus. Apparently, because more of you are coming than we had anticipated, the BW is now sold out. Try instead the Clarion University Hotel, which also has shuttle service to/from the airport and campus. You can book it at http://www.choicehotels.com/ires/en-US/html/HotelInfo?hotel=NY051. Note that there is also shuttle service from/to the Syracuse airport provided by Ithaca Airline Limosine (800) 273-9197 or (607) 273-3030, but you need an advance reservation. Parking on campus on Friday requires a permit which you can purchase at one of the parking booths. Please let us know if you are planning to stay there and whether or not you will have a car so that you might provide transportation, notably to the picnic Friday evening. Also, we will arrange further activities for those participants planning to stay over Saturday night, so please let us know if you wish to join us for dinner Saturday evening.
