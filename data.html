The ALFALFA Legacy

The ALFALFA Survey Scientific Products

This page is an updated public repository of datasets produced by the ALFALFA Team. ALFALFA data can be accessed as catalogs of detections, the HI spectra of specific targets, or by downloading an entire datacube of observations in a region of sky.

Catalogs

  • The extragalactic source catalog is also available through the Vizier service as J/ApJ/861/49.
  • The Archived Data Page contains older catalogs.

Spectra and Data Cubes

  • Official data cubes can be found here.
  • Spectra can be found as part of NED.
